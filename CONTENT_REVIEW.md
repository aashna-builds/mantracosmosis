# Content review checklist

Please have a native Hindi / Sanskrit reader (and, for the monk, a Tibetan reader) check these before a public release.

## Hindi explanations ("Gyan")
- Grammar, warmth and tone of every two-line Hindi note in `index.html` (search for `gyan:`).
- Least sure: the Tara note ("का रूप मानी जाती हैं") and the Bhairav note.

## Spelling choices
- हूँ (chandrabindu) versus हूं (anusvara) for "Hum".
- Tibetan: པདྨེ (current) versus པད་མེ for "padme".
- The last word of Mahamrityunjaya is written माऽमृतात् (with avagraha).

## Wording
- Meaning lines for Kali and Bhairav are deliberately restrained (seed sound, guardian). Confirm they read as respectful in each tradition.
- Tara is described as a goddess of compassion in Tibetan Buddhism and one of the ten Mahavidyas in Hindu tradition.

## Lip-sync timing
- Syllable splits, vowel choices and durations are in the `WORDS_*` lists near the top of the script. They were written by hand and can be tuned by someone who chants these.
